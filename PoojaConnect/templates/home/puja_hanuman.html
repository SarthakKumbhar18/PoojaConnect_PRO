Hanuman Puja

Hanuman Puja is a sacred Hindu ritual dedicated to Lord Hanuman, the symbol of strength, devotion, courage, and protection. Devotees worship Lord Hanuman to seek protection from negative energies, obstacles, and fears.

Lord Hanuman is known as the greatest devotee of Lord Rama and represents unwavering faith, dedication, and loyalty. Performing Hanuman Puja with devotion is believed to bring courage, success, and spiritual strength in life.

1. Introduction to Hanuman Puja

Hanuman Puja is performed to honor Lord Hanuman, the powerful deity known for his strength, devotion, and wisdom. Devotees pray to Lord Hanuman for courage, protection, and the removal of obstacles in life.

This puja is commonly performed on Tuesdays and Saturdays, which are considered especially auspicious for worshipping Lord Hanuman.

2. Essential Puja Samagri (Items Required for the Ritual)

  • Idol or image of Lord Hanuman
  • Red flowers
  • Vermilion (Sindoor)
  • Betel leaves and betel nuts
  • Incense sticks and camphor
  • Coconut
  • Bananas and fruits
  • Laddoos or sweets
  • Oil lamp (Diya)
  • Holy water (Gangajal)
  • Tulsi leaves

3. Purpose and Importance of Performing Hanuman Puja

  • To gain strength, courage, and confidence.
  • To remove fear, negativity, and evil influences.
  • To overcome obstacles and difficulties.
  • To improve mental strength and determination.
  • To seek protection and blessings from Lord Hanuman.

4. Historical and Religious Significance

Lord Hanuman plays a significant role in the epic Ramayana as the devoted follower of Lord Rama. His courage, loyalty, and devotion are celebrated across Hindu traditions.

Hanuman Puja reminds devotees of the values of strength, humility, and devotion. His worship is believed to remove suffering and bring divine protection to devotees.

5. Detailed Overview of the Hanuman Puja Ritual

The puja begins with lighting a lamp and offering prayers to Lord Hanuman. Devotees apply vermilion (Sindoor) to the idol and offer flowers, sweets, fruits, and incense.

Devotees chant powerful prayers such as the Hanuman Chalisa and Bajrang Baan to seek blessings. The puja concludes with aarti and distribution of prasad among devotees.

Regular worship of Lord Hanuman is believed to bring strength, courage, protection, and spiritual growth in life.

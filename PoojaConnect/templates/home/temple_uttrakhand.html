Famous Temples of Uttarakhand

Kedarnath Temple

Location: Kedarnath Get Directions

Timings: 4:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Seasonal)
About: Kedarnath Temple is one of the most sacred temples dedicated to Lord Shiva and is part of the 12 Jyotirlingas. Located in the Himalayas, it is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites and attracts lakhs of devotees every year.
Historical Significance: According to legend, the temple was built by the Pandavas and later revived by Adi Shankaracharya. It stands as a symbol of faith and devotion amidst extreme natural conditions.

Kainchi Dham

Location: Nainital Get Directions

Timings: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM
About: Kainchi Dham is a famous ashram dedicated to Neem Karoli Baba. It is known for its peaceful environment and spiritual energy, attracting devotees and visitors from around the world.
Historical Significance: The ashram was established in the 1960s by Neem Karoli Baba. Many well-known personalities like Steve Jobs and Mark Zuckerberg have visited this sacred place.

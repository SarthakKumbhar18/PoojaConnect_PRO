Famous Temples of Karnataka

Udupi Krishna Temple

Location: Udupi Get Directions

Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
About: Udupi Krishna Temple is one of the most famous temples dedicated to Lord Krishna, located in Udupi, Karnataka. The temple is known for its unique style of worship where devotees view the idol of Lord Krishna through a small silver window called "Kanakana Kindi." It is an important center of devotion and attracts thousands of devotees, especially followers of Lord Krishna and Vaishnavism.
Historical Significance: The temple was established by the great saint and philosopher Madhvacharya in the 13th century. It is a major center of Dvaita Vedanta philosophy. The temple has a rich history of religious practices and traditions, and it is also associated with the story of Kanakadasa, a devotee whose devotion is honored through the special window in the temple.

Virupaksha Temple

Location: Hampi Get Directions

Timings: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
About: Virupaksha Temple is one of the oldest and most important temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, located in Hampi, Karnataka. It is a major pilgrimage center and is still actively worshipped today. The temple is known for its grand architecture, towering gopuram (entrance tower), and intricate carvings that reflect the rich cultural heritage of South India.
Historical Significance: The temple dates back to the 7th century and gained prominence during the Vijayanagara Empire, where it became a central place of worship. It is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site at Hampi and stands as a symbol of the architectural and spiritual excellence of ancient India.

Mookambika Temple

Location: Kollur Get Directions

Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
About: Kollur Mookambika Temple is a famous temple dedicated to Goddess Mookambika, located in Kollur, Karnataka. The goddess is worshipped as a combined form of Saraswati, Lakshmi, and Parvati, symbolizing knowledge, wealth, and power. The temple is especially popular among students and devotees seeking blessings for education, success, and spiritual growth.
Historical Significance: According to tradition, the temple was established by the great philosopher Adi Shankaracharya. It is believed that the goddess appeared to him in a divine vision, and he consecrated the idol at this sacred place. The temple has been an important spiritual center for centuries and continues to attract thousands of devotees from across India.

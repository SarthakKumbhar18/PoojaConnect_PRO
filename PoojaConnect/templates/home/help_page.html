How Users Can Use PoojaConnect

Step 1

Create an account by registering as a user.

Step 2

Browse pandits and available pooja services.

Step 3

Send a booking request to the pandit.

Step 4

Complete the payment securely using Razorpay.

Step 5

Attend pooja via video call or in-person service.

Step 6

View payment history, invoices, and receive notifications.

How Pandits Can Use PoojaConnect

Step 1

Register as a Pandit and create your profile.

Step 2

Add your experience, specialization, and location.

Step 3

Receive pooja booking requests from users.

Step 4

Accept or reject booking requests.

Step 5

Generate invoices and conduct pooja via video or offline.

Step 6

Track payments, chat with users, and receive notifications.

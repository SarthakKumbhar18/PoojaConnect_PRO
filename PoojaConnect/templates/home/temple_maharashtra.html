Famous Temples of Maharashtra

Shirdi Sai Baba Temple

Location: Shirdi Get Directions

Timings: 4:00 AM – 11:00 PM
About: Shirdi Sai Baba Temple is one of the most visited spiritual destinations in India. It is dedicated to Sai Baba, who is worshipped as a saint and spiritual guide by millions of devotees. The temple attracts people from all religions and promotes unity, faith, and devotion.
Historical Significance: Sai Baba lived in Shirdi during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. His teachings focused on love, forgiveness, and helping others. After his samadhi, the temple was built and has become one of the richest and most visited temples in India.

Siddhivinayak Temple

Location: Mumbai Get Directions

Timings: 5:30 AM – 9:50 PM
About: Siddhivinayak Temple is one of the most famous temples of Lord Ganesha in India. Devotees visit this temple to seek blessings for success, prosperity, and removal of obstacles.
Historical Significance: The temple was built in 1801 and has grown into a major religious center. It is especially popular among celebrities and politicians, and is considered very powerful and auspicious.

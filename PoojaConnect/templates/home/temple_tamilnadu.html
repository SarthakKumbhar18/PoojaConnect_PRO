Famous Temples of Tamil Nadu

Meenakshi Amman Temple

Location: Madurai Get Directions

Timings: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM
About: Meenakshi Temple is one of the most iconic temples in India, dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi (Parvati) and Lord Sundareswarar (Shiva). The temple is famous for its colorful gopurams, intricate carvings, and grand architecture.
Historical Significance: The temple dates back to ancient times and was expanded during the Nayak dynasty. It represents the cultural and architectural brilliance of Dravidian temple style and is a major pilgrimage center.

Ramanathaswamy Temple

Location: Rameshwaram Get Directions

Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
About: Ramanathaswamy Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlinga temples of Lord Shiva and an important pilgrimage site. It is also part of the Char Dham Yatra.
Historical Significance: According to Hindu mythology, Lord Rama worshipped Lord Shiva here before crossing to Lanka. The temple is known for its longest corridor among temples in India.
